The reason we built it

This is Ye-Ye — James's grandfather. He was diagnosed with ALS, and over time, the disease stole his ability to speak. The solutions available to him were too slow, too expensive, or required surgery. Ye-Ye passed away without a device that could keep up with his thoughts.

We knew there had to be a better way.
